🎂 Happy Cake – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Happy Cake ("the game", "we") is a casual cake-stacking game that runs as a Telegram Mini App via @cakestack_bot. This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

1. Data we collect

  • Telegram profile data that Telegram shares with Mini Apps: your Telegram user ID, first and last name, username, profile photo link and language.
  • Game data: your scores, best score, number of games played, play time per game, daily challenge progress and streak, and free revives.
  • Invite data: who invited you (if you joined through an invite link), how many friends you invited, and the campaign source of the link you used (for example "tiktok").
  • Christmas Cup data: your best score each week, your weekly rank and the prizes you received.
  • Purchase data: what you bought in the Shop, the price in Telegram Stars, the date and the Telegram payment ID (needed for refunds). We never receive your card or payment details; payments are handled by Telegram.
  • Bot messages: whether you started the bot or allowed it to message you, your language, and whether you turned weekly messages off with /stop.
  • Device storage: small settings such as your best score and sound preference are saved in Telegram Cloud Storage or your device.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game, save your progress and show the Top 20 leaderboard.
  • To give invite rewards, daily challenge rewards and Christmas Cup prizes, and to prevent cheating.
  • To deliver purchased items, handle payment support and refunds.
  • To send you the weekly Christmas Cup results through the bot, if you started the bot or allowed it to message you. Send /stop to turn this off.
  • To understand how players find and use the game, so we can improve it.

3. What other players can see

If you appear on the leaderboard or the Christmas Cup board, other players can see your Telegram name, username, profile photo, best score and a flag based on your Telegram language. Your Telegram user ID is never shown.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase stores game and leaderboard data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game files.
  • Telegram processes payments in Telegram Stars under its own terms.
  • AdsGram shows optional rewarded video ads. AdsGram may process data under its own privacy policy.
  • Telegram Analytics (by TON) measures app launches and usage.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep game data while the game is running. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing tranhien106@gmail.com with your Telegram username. We will remove it within 30 days.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but we do not knowingly collect more data from children than described above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
